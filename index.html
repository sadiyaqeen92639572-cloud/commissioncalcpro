FAQ

Do sellers still have to pay the buyer's agent commission?

No, not automatically. Since the NAR settlement took effect on August 17, 2024, buyer-agent compensation was removed from MLS listings and is now negotiated separately between the buyer and their agent. Roughly 35% of sellers still choose to cover some or all of it as a negotiating concession, but it is optional, not standard.

What is the average real estate commission rate?

There is no single official average — NAR doesn't publish one. Clever Real Estate's Feb 2026 survey (n=533 agents) found 5.70% national average, ranging from 4.50% in DC to 6.20% in Michigan. FastExpert's early-2025 survey (n=580 agents) found 5.57%. Treat any single number with that in mind — see the full state-by-state ranges.

How is commission split between agent and broker?

It varies by brokerage — a common split is 70/30 in the agent's favor, but traditional brokerages often keep 30-50%. Use the split calculator to model your specific split.
